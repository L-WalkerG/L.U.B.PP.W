Caspian Drift: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Caspian Drift ("the game") is made by Level Up Baku. This policy explains what the game stores and shares. Contact: levelupbakugames@gmail.com

What the game stores on your device

  • Progress and settings: scores, coins, cars, paints, upgrades, tuning, car mastery, career
  • stars, missions, awards, login streak, Season Road progress, your Weekly Cup result and your
  • settings (language, controls, sound, accessibility, reminders, where you put the horn button).
  • Local records: your best runs for each game mode.
  • Gameplay statistics: anonymous events such as "a run started" or "a run ended with this
  • score on this route", plus totals. They contain no name, account, contact details,
  • location or device identifiers. You can turn them off in Settings → Analytics.
  • Error reports: when something goes wrong in the game, a short technical note: the error
  • message, where in the game's code it happened, the game version and the operating system name
  • (for example "Android"). The game also notes if it closed unexpectedly. These contain no
  • personal information and follow the same Analytics setting.

These game records stay on your device. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include them in your device backup to your own Google account, so your progress can come back on a new phone or after reinstalling; Android handles that backup and we can't see it. Clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the game to delete the records on the phone (and turn off backup or delete the backup in your Google account to remove that copy). Images exported outside the app’s storage remain until you delete them separately.

What the game sends

The game has no accounts of its own and does not send your progress, statistics or error reports to us.

Ads (Google AdMob). After a crash you can choose to watch a video ad to keep driving; ads never play unless you pick them. These ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and use your device’s advertising ID and app set ID, IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), app/ad interactions and SDK diagnostic information, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and other places where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy options. The SDK may initialize and preload an ad after the consent flow permits ad requests, before you choose to watch a video. Turning off the game’s local Analytics setting does not turn off the advertising SDK’s data handling. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services). The game can show global leaderboards through Google Play Games. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game sends your scores to Google so they appear on the game's leaderboards, together with your Play Games profile (your gamer name and avatar). Other players can see your scores and profile on those leaderboards, depending on the profile visibility you choose in Play Games ("Everyone", "Friends only" or "Only you"). The Play Games software also collects analytics and diagnostics data to keep its service working and improve it, and sends data over an encrypted connection. Sign-in is handled by Google Play Games: the game never receives your email address or password. You can play without signing in; your records then stay on your device only. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and its game data at https://play.google.com/games/profile or in your Google Account (https://myaccount.google.com). Google's use of this data is described in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Reminders

This version does not deliver notifications or request notification permission. Reminder scheduling is not enabled in the shipped Android app.

Rating the game

After you have played for a while, the game may ask whether you'd like to rate it. Choosing to rate opens the game's Google Play page; your rating and review go to Google Play under its own terms. The game only remembers whether it has already asked.

Sharing your record

When you choose to share a record, the game saves an image on your device and/or opens WhatsApp with a message you can edit before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: for haptic feedback; can be turned off in Settings.
  • Internet / network state: for Google AdMob and its consent forms, and for Google Play
  • Games leaderboards.
  • Advertising ID: used by the advertising SDK, subject to your device settings and applicable consent choices.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It does not ask players for their name, age or contact information. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that shared their data through ads or Google Play Games, contact us at the address above and we will help.

Changes

We will update this page when the game's data practices change.
